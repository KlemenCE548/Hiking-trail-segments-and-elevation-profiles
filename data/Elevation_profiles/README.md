Title:
StackProfileResults

Description:
Elevation profiles derived from the 1 m Digital Terrain Model (DTM) along 25 hiking trail segments used in the associated PLOS ONE study. The dataset is based on the output generated by Script 4 of the analysis workflow and was subsequently formatted for publication.

The elevation-profile data were used in the calculation of the vertical fractal dimensions reported in the study.

File:
StackProfileResults.csv

Columns:
OBJECTID    Unique record identifier.
ID_Path     Identifier of the corresponding hiking trail segment.
Distance    Distance along the profile (m).
Elevation   Elevation (m a.s.l.).

Software:
ArcGIS Pro 3.6

Authors:
Klemen Prah, Simona Šinko, and Ashton M. Shortridge

Date:
November 2026
